2. Eligibility & Condition Requirements

To ensure hygiene and safety for all customers, returned items must meet the condition guidelines below:

Eligible for Return

  • Unwashed & Unused Furnishings: Cushions, curtains, and linens must be unwashed, unworn, and free from pet hair, smoke, or perfume odors.
  • Original Packaging & Tags: Items must include original brand tags, care labels, zippered storage bags, and protective wrapping.
  • Clean Mattresses (100-Night Trial): Mattresses may be unboxed and slept on, provided they are free of stains, tears, burns, or structural misuse.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Custom Foam Cuts: High-density foam sheets, window seat pads, or bolsters cut to custom dimensions specified by the buyer.
  • Bespoke Upholstery & Cut Fabrics: Custom-stitched sofa covers, tailored drapery, or upholstery fabric cut from the roll by the meter/yard.
  • Hygiene & Clearance Items: Opened mattress protectors, pillow protectors, fabric swatch kits, and items marked "Final Sale".

3. Step-by-Step Return Process

Follow these four simple steps to send your item back to us smoothly:

01

Submit Return Request

Click "Start a Return" on this page or email our support team with your Order ID, item name, and reason for return. Attach photos if reporting a defect.

02

Approval & Pickup Scheduling

Within 24 business hours, we will verify eligibility and email you a prepaid return shipping label or confirm a scheduled doorstep courier pickup slot.

03

Pack Securely for Transit

Place furnishings back in their original packaging with tags attached. For mattresses, no need to re-compress—our logistics partner will wrap it at pickup.

04

Quality Check & Refund

Once your return arrives at our warehouse and passes a quick quality inspection (1–2 business days), your refund or exchange is immediately released.

5. Exchanges & Damaged or Defective Items

Size, Firmness & Color Exchanges

Need a softer pillow loft, a different cushion cover shade, or a firmer mattress feel? We offer one complimentary exchange per order within your return window. If the replacement item has a price difference, we will either refund the balance or send a secure payment link for the difference.

Damaged in Transit or Manufacturing Defects

Every piece from Wish Foam & Furnishing Co undergoes strict quality checks, including our custom foam and upholstery orders. However, if your order arrives with transit damage, incorrect dimensions, or a stitching/foam defect:

  • Report the issue within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos of the item and outer packaging.
  • We will arrange a 100% free replacement or full refund—including for custom-cut foam and bespoke upholstery.
  • Long-term foam sagging (beyond 1.5 inches) is covered separately under our 5-to-10 Year Mattress & Foam Warranty.
